Why AKCO

We came from control rooms,
not showrooms

01

Background

Twenty-plus years of operating industrial automation taught us to analyze the asset, find the real sources of trouble and of efficiency gains, and pick the right solutions from a crowded field. Analyze first, understand the root cause, then propose.

02

Understanding

We know the recurring problems of the areas we serve — we call them domain pains — from minor daily glitches to failures that stop entire plants, because we spent years steeped in them as operators. Every ecosystem grew out of a pain we know by name.

03

Co-engineering

Our way is to become part of your team, work on one site and in one information space, and earn our bonuses on results we achieve together. Terms and incentives are written into the contract, not left in the slide deck.

04

Accountable scope

A systems approach means we own the full circuit of pain removal — from the feasibility study through implementation to keeping the solution healthy across its whole lifecycle. “Not our department” is not spoken here.

05

Proven inside

Nearly every ecosystem runs its full lifecycle inside the Concern before we offer it to the market. The AI agents that answer calls, draft proposals, and write code are the very ones our own people use today. We offer a working system, not a render.

06

Openness

We actively use third-party technology and welcome the integration of external solutions into our ecosystems. Our principle: the best tool for removing the pain should win the job — even an outside one.

Manifesto

Concerto for Industry

Every project is a score. Sensors, control loops, users, engineers, economics, ecology — everything must play in tune, or the false note is heard: downtime, an incident, lost profit, a regulator's citation, wasted investment.

Most suppliers play their own part. One sells sensors, another pumps, a third software, a fourth knowledge. Each part may be excellent on its own. But no one answers for how it all sounds together — and the client is left holding a conductor's baton nobody asked for.

AKCO is a Concern. From the Latin concernere — “to share one matter.” From the Italian concerto — “to play in concert.” We are not a vendor of goods. We are a co-performer.

We join your team from the inside. We work on your sites. We earn our bonuses on your achieved KPIs, not on sales. We leave when your pain leaves — and we stay until it does.

From our own practice we assembled a full ensemble of intelligent instruments: AKSense™ detects the signals, AKFlow™ manages the flows, AKCore™ thinks through the execution, AK.COmm teams deliver the projects, and AKNeuro™ looks into humanity's future. Each can play solo. Together they sound like concernere — one language, one score.

We are an engineering school raised on problems that are not in the textbooks. We are the Concern that works with you, not instead of you.
